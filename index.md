2. What this app does

This app connects Meta services (Instagram messaging) to an n8n automation workflow.

We use it to test an automated assistant that answers customer messages on a business’s Instagram account. The assistant replies to questions about products, prices, and availability. When it cannot answer, it asks the business owner for help and then sends the owner’s answer back to the customer.

3. Information we process

Depending on the Meta service and permissions used, our app processes:

The text of messages sent to the connected Instagram account
The sender’s Instagram-scoped user ID and the account’s ID
Message timestamps and event details
Images or voice messages, only if the sender chooses to send them

Our workflow may also store this information in execution logs when recording workflow activity or troubleshooting errors.

4. How we use information

We use this information to operate the workflow described above, receive webhook events, test the integration, and fix errors. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Storage and other services

The workflow runs on n8n (self-hosted) on a computer operated by the app owner. It is connected to the internet through a Cloudflare Tunnel.

Information is also sent to:

Google (Google Sheets, to look up product information and record questions waiting for the owner’s answer)
Google (Gemini AI, to write replies)
Telegram (to notify the business owner)

These services process the information needed to run the workflow.

6. How long we keep information

We keep webhook data and workflow logs only for as long as needed for testing and fixing errors. We remove them when you ask us to (see Section 7).
